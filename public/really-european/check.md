# Is it really European? Ownership checker

Analytics Ascent research desk. HTML version: https://analyticascent.com/really-european/check

Type a product or company name. The checker searches our 216-row dataset (checked 2026-10-18) in your browser and shows the legal entity, country, LEI or "none", the parent GLEIF reports or the reporting exception, the owner a primary document names, the source link and the date we checked it.

Verdicts: EU-controlled, non-EU-controlled, PE-controlled, or unverified. A verdict other than unverified always has a primary document behind it.

Optional live lookup: for names not in the dataset, the page can query the GLEIF API (api.gleif.org, CC0) from your browser, one request at a time. A GLEIF parent is what the company reported about itself; the checker never turns it into a verdict, so live results are always shown as unverified.

Nothing you type is sent to Analytics Ascent.

Dataset: https://analyticascent.com/data/really-european.csv (CC0). Method and full table: https://analyticascent.com/really-european.md
