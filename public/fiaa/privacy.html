Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy covers the FIAA website and the private publishing tool FIAA uses to post its own videos to Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

What we collect

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. The publishing tool signs in only to accounts owned by FIAA. Through the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API it uploads FIAA videos and reads the upload status. It does not read, store or share data about any other user.

Sign-in tokens

The access tokens Google and TikTok issue to the tool are stored as encrypted secrets in our private code repository and are used only to publish FIAA videos. You can see and revoke the tool's access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube content is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. We do not collect personal data from people who watch our videos beyond what each platform shows every account owner.
