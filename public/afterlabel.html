Where do I find these files?

UPS: sign in to UPS Billing Center, open an invoice (or a date range) and download the billing data as CSV. Files with or without a header row both work.

Shopify: Orders, then Export, choose the date range that matches the invoice, CSV format.

ShipStation: Shipments, then Export. It links tracking numbers to order numbers.
